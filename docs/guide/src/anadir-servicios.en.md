With the shipment data complete, click **[[Añadir servicios]]** (Add services) and choose the services (THC, PAD,
BL, DEM…).

- Each service creates **one line in its charge basis** (container, shipment, B/L, chargeable kg…).
- **Container** services get one line per **size and type** present in the shipment (2 × 20' DV and 1 × 40' HC →
  two lines), because suppliers and customers may price them differently.
- The **quantity** comes from the shipment (containers, TEU, kg, m³, pallets) and follows changes of the shipment
  data. Per-document or per-hour services are counted on the line.
- You can also add lines by hand with [[Añadir línea]]; the calculation is the same.

> The yellow banner sums up what is missing in the whole quotation. Columns hidden by default (container type, sale
> status and VAT) are shown with the column icon on the right of the table.
