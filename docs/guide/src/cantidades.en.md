The **charge basis** (unit of the line) says what is sold and priced: container, TEU, shipment, B/L, AWB, customs
declaration, document, gross kg, chargeable kg air/road, tonne, m³, W/M, pallet, hour. There is **no conversion** between
bases: a per-container price never applies to a per-W/M line.

The supplier price also says **what it charges on** ("Priced on"), read from the document:

| Priced on | Value |
|---|---|
| Quantity of the line | The line quantity (documents, hours, entered kg…) |
| Containers of the line | Shipment containers of the line's size and type |
| TEU of the containers | 20' = 1, 40' and 45' = 2 |
| Gross weight (kg / tonnes), volume (m³) | Shipment data |
| W/M | The larger of tonnes and m³ |
| Chargeable kg (document factor) | The larger of kg and m³ × factor (e.g. 167 air, 333 road) — the factor is written on the price |
| Chargeable kg of the air waybill | The chargeable weight on the AWB (when the document gives no factor) |
| Pallets, kilometres | Shipment or line data |

**Unit size** and **or fraction**: "per 100 kg or fraction" = size 100 and rounding up (450 kg → 5).

> Chargeable-weight factors are data of each document; when a document does not state one, the AWB chargeable weight
> is used (P14).
